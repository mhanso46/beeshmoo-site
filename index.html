How it works

Three small steps, every week.

1

Add your people

Put each person in a circle: family, loved ones, friends, coworkers. Closer circles get checked on more often.

2

Get a gentle nudge

Each week Beeshmoo shows who's due. No streaks, no guilt. Busy week? Tap "Not this week" and it waits.

3

Have a real conversation

Open their page for a thoughtful question or the news you jotted down last time, so a quick text turns into a real catch-up.

Circles

Everyone has a rhythm.

Your mom doesn't need the same reminder as your old college roommate. Beeshmoo gives each circle its own pace, from every week to every couple of months.

There's even a circle for the people who take care of you, like your stylist or massage therapist, with reminders to book your next visit.

What's inside

Small things that add up.

Gentle reminders

See who's due this week. Reached out and waiting? Beeshmoo remembers and nudges you to follow up.

Plans and birthdays

Coffee on Saturday or "sometime in January." Beeshmoo nudges you when it's time to pick a date.

Things to talk about

Thoughtful questions and fresh ideas each week, picked for each person.

Remember the details

Kids' names, a new job, a favorite restaurant, a gift idea. Jot it once and it's there next time.

Private by design

What you put in Beeshmoo is yours. No ads, no selling your information, and the people you add never hear from us. Read the privacy policy.
